A LETTER FOR YOU

To my wife, my favorite person. ❤️

Happy Birthday, my love. ❤️

Today is not just your birthday to me. It is a reminder of how lucky I am to have you in my life. Among so many people in this world, I somehow found you, and you became the person who means the most to me.

We have been through so many things together. We have laughed, fought, misunderstood each other, cried, and sometimes even felt like everything was becoming too difficult. But somehow, we always found our way back to each other. And honestly, that is one of the things I love most about us.

You were there for me when life was not easy. When I was confused about my future and going through difficult times, you believed in me. There were moments when I felt like giving up, but your support gave me strength. You didn't just love me when everything was perfect; you stayed when things were difficult too. I will always be grateful for that.

I know I am not perfect. Sometimes I get angry, sometimes I make mistakes, sometimes I fail to express what I feel, and sometimes I may hurt you without even realizing it. But please always remember one thing: my love for you is real. No matter how many small fights we have or how difficult a day becomes, I never want to lose you.

You are not only my wife. You are my best friend, my comfort, my safe place, and the person I want to share my whole life with. ❤️

I don't know what the future will look like. Maybe there will be more challenges, more responsibilities, and more difficult days. But I know one thing for sure — I want to face them with you. I want to see you achieve your dreams. I want to make you proud. I want to build a beautiful life with you, step by step.

On your birthday, I don't want to wish you only happiness for today. I wish you a life full of happiness, peace, success, good health, and beautiful moments. I hope every dream you have slowly becomes real. And whenever life becomes difficult, I hope you remember that you will always have someone beside you who loves you deeply.

Thank you for choosing me. Thank you for staying with me. Thank you for believing in me when I needed it the most. And thank you for becoming such an important part of my life.

I may not always say it perfectly, but I love you more than I can explain with words. ❤️

Happy Birthday to the woman I love, the woman I chose, and the woman I want beside me for the rest of my life. 🥹❤️

May this new year of your life bring you everything your heart wishes for.

And no matter how old we get, I hope we still celebrate birthdays together, laugh about our old memories, fight over silly things, make up, and keep choosing each other again and again.

I love you today, I will love you tomorrow, and I will keep loving you through every chapter of our life.

Happy Birthday, my wife. ❤️
Forever yours.
